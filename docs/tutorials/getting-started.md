# Getting started

From a Pocket Query to your first walked power trail. You need a
Geocaching.com Premium membership (Pocket Queries are a Premium feature) and
a phone with a current browser.

## 1. Create a Pocket Query

On geocaching.com, open **Pocket Queries** and create a new one:

- **Cache types**: Traditional only. Power trails are almost always
  Traditionals, and fewer caches means a faster import.
- **That I haven't found** and **Is enabled**, so the trails are made of
  caches you can still log.
- **Difficulty** and **Terrain** up to 2, for quick finds.
- **Within** a radius around the place you want to walk, small enough that
  the query stays at or below **1,000 caches**.
- Tick **Compress files into a .zip**.

Run it. Queries with up to 500 results are also e-mailed to you.

## 2. Get the file

Once the query has run, download its `.zip` from the Pocket Queries page
("Ready for download"), or open the e-mail it sent. Keep the file as it is;
there is no need to unzip it.

## 3. Open the app and import

Open PowerTrailBuddy and choose **Import**, then pick the `.zip` (or the
`.gpx` files). On Android, after installing the app (step 6), you can also
**share** the file to PowerTrailBuddy straight from your e-mail or files app.

The app reads the caches into your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

## 4. Pick a trail

The app lists the trails it found, best first: caches per hour, number of
caches, walking distance and time. Open one to see it on the map.

Nothing found? Widen the limits in **Settings** — a larger gap between
caches, fewer caches per trail — or import a query for a denser area.

## 5. Walk it

Start the walk. The app shows the next cache with its distance and
direction; mark each one **found** or **skip** it. Your progress is kept if
you close the app, and caches you mark found are left out of future trails.

## 6. Install it

In your browser's menu choose **Install app** (Android/Chrome) or **Add to
Home Screen** (iOS/Safari). Installed, PowerTrailBuddy opens like any other
app, works offline once loaded, and on Android appears in the share sheet.
